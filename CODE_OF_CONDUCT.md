# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone participating in issues, discussions, pull requests, and other project
spaces is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be welcoming and patient. This is a beginner project: no question is too
  basic, and nobody should be mocked for not knowing something yet.
- Be respectful, even in disagreement. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualized content, or personal attacks.
- **Security-specific:** never post real people's credentials, private data,
  or targets you aren't authorized to test, and don't ask for help attacking
  systems you don't own. Such content will be removed.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/TMHSDigital/Kali-Linux-for-beginners/security/advisories/new)
(select "Code of Conduct" in the title), or by contacting the
[@TMHSDigital](https://github.com/TMHSDigital) maintainers directly. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this code, as
described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
